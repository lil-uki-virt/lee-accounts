Answers

What are Lee Accounts?
A one-time $1,000 contribution from Uncle Kevin for each of his nieces and his nephew, held for their long-term benefit. One child, one account, one thousand dollars.
Why?
Uncle Kevin sold some Apple stock, and would rather spread it around than leave it sitting in one account. The three of them have several more decades of compounding ahead of them than he does.
Who is eligible?
Every niece and nephew of Kevin Lee, aka Desmond, Rose and Tilden.
Are contributions from relatives tax-deductible?
No. These are personal gifts, not charitable contributions.
